#### Q1: "Why not just use ChatGPT or Canva with Zapier to generate carousels?"
> **Your Answer:**  
> *"Because Canva and Zapier rely on static bounding boxes and ChatGPT cannot compute pixel coordinates. When copy length changes by two sentences, Canva either shrinks text to illegible sizes or truncates it. Our architecture separates the LLM from the layout: Claude handles brief ingestion and typography hierarchy, while a headless HTML/CSS engine computes exact layout math. It gives your agency total brand locking across 200+ clients with zero manual Figma adjustments."*

#### Q2: "Our video editors hate AI tools that output finished MP4s because they can't tweak the cuts. How does your pipeline solve this?"

When your editor opens DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro, the project opens with all the media linked, dead air already ripple-cut, and candidate hooks organized on tracks. Your editors maintain 100% creative control to fine-tune color, pacing, and sound design."*

#### Q3: "How do you handle team adoption and training so our staff actually uses it?"

### B. US Sports Tech Interview Questions (Automated Sports Mixtape Platform)

#### Q1: "How do you guarantee cuts and zooms hit the music beat accurately without manual keyframing?"
> **Your Answer:**  
> *"We use Remotion’s programmatic `@remotion/media-utils` engine with `getAudioData()`. Instead of relying on a human editor tapping markers in Premiere, our code analyzes the music stem's raw PCM audio buffers. We apply digital low-pass filtering to isolate sub-bass kick/808 spikes (30–120Hz). When the amplitude crosses our dynamic energy threshold, Remotion automatically snaps the video transition or triggers an overshoot spring zoom on that exact frame."*

#### Q2: "How will this render fast enough if we have hundreds of amateur athletes ordering mixtapes on Friday night?"
> **Your Answer:**  
> *"A single server rendering sequentially would crash under that load. We deploy the pipeline using **Remotion Lambda** across AWS serverless infrastructure. When a 60-second mixtape is requested, Remotion distributes the composition across 32 to 64 Lambda workers in parallel. Each worker renders 1 to 2 seconds of frames in 4 seconds, and FFmpeg concatenates the chunks into an H.264 MP4 in 3 seconds. Total render turnaround is under 25 seconds per video, costing pennies on AWS."*

#### Q3: "In sports footage, audio is often chaotic with whistles, parents screaming, and crowd noise. How do you handle audio balance?"
> **Your Answer:**  
> *"We apply an automated multi-stage FFmpeg filter chain. First, `afftdn` FFT noise profiling eliminates low room rumble and wind noise. Second, our audio ducking uses deterministic lookahead curves rather than reactive analog compressors, so the music bed smoothly ducks by -10dB during commentary without any distracting 'breathing' or volume pumping. Finally, FFmpeg's `loudnorm` filter ensures every export complies strictly with the -14 LUFS standard for TikTok and YouTube Shorts."*
