*   Competitor 5 (Auto-Bath) loads instantly. The user drags their finger on their phone, and a 3D car spins in real-time. 

**The Psychological Result:** The customer instantly assumes Auto-Bath is the most expensive, most elite, and most trustworthy option on the market. We win the job before they even read the price.

## 5. Google SEO & Local Ranking Superiority (Proven Best Practices)

To dominate the Melbourne local search results (Google Maps and Organic Search), we must execute technical and local SEO flawlessly. Since we are using Next.js, we inherently beat 90% of competitors on technical speed, but we must pair it with strict content practices.

### A. Technical SEO Supremacy (The Next.js Advantage)
*   **Core Web Vitals:** Google’s algorithm heavily penalizes slow websites. Our Headless Next.js architecture guarantees "Green" scores across LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) and CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift).
*   **Semantic HTML & Schema Markup:** We will inject `LocalBusiness` and `AutoBodyShop` JSON-LD Schema directly into the `<head>`. This mathematically tells Google exactly where the business is, what it does, and how much it charges, forcing rich snippets to appear in search results.
*   **Server-Side Metadata:** Every single service page (e.g., `/services/ceramic-coating-melbourne`) will have server-rendered `<title>` and `<meta description>` tags dynamically optimized for exact-match keywords.

### B. Local SEO & The Google Business Profile (GBP) Strategy
*   **Review Velocity:** Google ranks businesses not just on the *number* of reviews, but how *frequently* they come in. Our automated T+24hr CRM SMS ensures a steady, weekly stream of 5-star reviews, which signals massive trust to Google's local algorithm.
*   **NAP Consistency:** We will ensure the Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP) on the website's footer perfectly matches the Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, and local Australian directories down to the exact comma.
*   **Geo-Targeted Content:** We will embed the **Mapbox 3D Map** and use localized copywriting (e.g., "Premium Ceramic Coating in Melbourne's CBD") to trigger local proximity searches when high-end clients search near the shop.
